## Who I am and what project I am working on
I am Akhil Dhyani, and for my Summer of Bitcoin 2026 project, I am working on the **SeedSigner**. SeedSigner is an open-source, air-gapped Bitcoin signing device that typically runs on a Raspberry Pi Zero with a 240x240 pixel LCD screen and a camera.

My project focuses on building an alternative **Constrained UI Runner** that allows the entire SeedSigner software stack to run on vastly smaller, cheaper hardware displays (like 16x2 character LCDs and 128x32 OLEDs), while also establishing a MicroSD fallback pipeline for environments where cameras are unavailable.
